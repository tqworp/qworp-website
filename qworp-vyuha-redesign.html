Etymology

व्यूह — an arrangement built to win.

In the Mahabharata, a vyuha was a battle formation: troops arranged with such precision that only those who understood its geometry could enter, act and exit without being lost inside it.

Markets aren't so different. They reward the trader who sees the structure others miss, and quietly consume the one who wanders in without one.

This is the structure Vyuha builds into capital — a systematic formation, in a market built to overwhelm the undisciplined.
